# Access-verification report: Germany

Research date: 6 September 2026. “Current robots” means the publisher’s robots.txt as available on that date. Historical paywall conclusions are based on 2024 article samples and publisher descriptions. Coverage shares behind paywalls are marked UNVERIFIED where no complete archive enumeration was possible.

## Tagesschau (ARD)

1. **Identity in 2024.** News articles were published on `tagesschau.de`, mainly under paths such as `/ausland/asien/`. The editorial product was ARD-aktuell’s Tagesschau service, not `ard.de`. [ARD-aktuell description](https://www.tagesschau.de/ueber-uns)

2. **Test URL.** [“Hisbollah-Miliz im Libanon: Tote und Tausende Verletzte nach Pager-Explosionen”](https://www.tagesschau.de/ausland/asien/libanon-hisbollah-pager-102.html), published 17 September 2024 at 19:49. It remains accessible.

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** None. Tagesschau.de was free and publicly funded. Breaking-news text and video were normally accessible without registration. The share of Lebanon items removed or restricted for rights reasons is UNVERIFIED.

4. **Robots/TDM.** Current robots.txt allows ordinary article crawling except specified paths, but blocks several AI crawlers, including GPTBot, Google-Extended, CCBot and ClaudeBot. It also expressly reserves the right to prohibit text and data mining under §44b(3) UrhG and prohibits AI-model training without written consent. [robots.txt](https://www.tagesschau.de/robots.txt)

5. **Enumeration hooks.** Tagesschau has stable section URLs, liveblogs and numeric-looking article slugs, but no clearly exposed complete September-October 2024 article archive was verified. Search-engine indexing and topic pages are useful, but exhaustive enumeration is UNVERIFIED.

6. **Depublizierung.** The 17 September article is still online today, so it was not removed under a blanket rule. ARD describes different retention periods for videos, including two years for news and documentaries, while some archival programming can remain permanently available. Article-level retention rules for every 2024 text are UNVERIFIED. [ARD retention rules](https://www.ard.de/die-ard/ard-programmdirektion/ueber-uns/verweildauer-102.html)

## ZDF heute / ZDFheute

1. **Identity in 2024.** The online news brand was `zdfheute.de`. It was not primarily `zdf.de/nachrichten`, although ZDF corporate and media pages remained on `zdf.de`. ZDF’s own press material identifies ZDFheute.de as the online news service. [ZDF heute press information](https://presseportal.zdf.de/pressemappe/heute-nachrichtenfamilie)

2. **Test URL.** [“Libanon: Tote durch Pager-Explosionen”](https://www.zdfheute.de/video/heute-nachrichten/libanon-pager-explosionen-video-100.html), published 17 September 2024 at 18:41. The page remains accessible.

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** None. ZDFheute was free and publicly funded. Breaking-news articles and clips were generally accessible without registration. The exact share of Lebanon coverage that may have been deleted for rights reasons is UNVERIFIED.

4. **Robots/TDM.** The current `zdf.de` robots file blocks a long list of crawlers, including GPTBot, CCBot, Google-Extended and ChatGPT-User. No separate ZDFheute robots file or explicit §44b TDM reservation was verified. [ZDF robots.txt](https://www.zdf.de/robots.txt)

5. **Enumeration hooks.** ZDFheute uses stable article paths with a terminal `-100.html` identifier, plus topic pages such as [the Lebanon topic page](https://www.zdfheute.de/thema/libanon-116.html). Topic pages expose dated historical items, but a complete date-indexed September-October archive was not verified.

6. **Depublizierung.** The 17 September 2024 video page remains online today. That establishes continued availability of at least this item, not universal retention. ZDF’s formal telemedia concept governs availability and rights periods, but a complete article-by-article depublizierung list for September 2024 is UNVERIFIED. [ZDF telemedia concept](https://www.zdf.de/assets/beschlussvorlage-fortschreibung-telemedienkonzept-zdf-100~original)

## RTL Aktuell / RTL News

1. **Identity in 2024.** Text news lived on `rtl.de`, with both `/news/` and legacy `/cms/` URL structures. RTL Aktuell is a television service; its text version was not hosted on ntv.de. RTL also distributed related audio and video through RTL+.

2. **Test URL.** [“Mysteriöse Explosionen von Funkempfängern: acht Tote und tausende Verletzte!”](https://www.rtl.de/news/explosionen-von-pagern-im-libanon-hisbollah-miliz-wirft-israel-anschlaege-vor-id1833651.html), published 17 September 2024 at 18:42. The article credits Reuters, dpa and other agency material.

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** None identified for ordinary RTL.de news. RTL’s commercial subscription products concerned video and entertainment, not a general RTL.de news paywall. Breaking-news text was typically free. The precise share of Lebanon coverage behind any registration or product gate is UNVERIFIED.

4. **Robots/TDM.** A current RTL robots.txt file could not be fetched reliably through the search tool. UNVERIFIED. RTL’s public RSS page confirms that its news feeds were intended to distribute current RTL.de articles freely. [RTL RSS information](https://www.rtl.de/cms/rss-feed-abonnieren-sie-die-rtl-de-auf-ihrem-feedreader-4476976.html)

5. **Enumeration hooks.** RTL exposes RSS feeds, including a general news feed and a politics feed. Older articles use numeric IDs in the URL, such as `id1833651`. A complete dated RTL archive for September-October 2024 was not verified. [RTL news RSS details](https://www.rtl.de/cms/rss-feed-abonnieren-sie-die-rtl-de-auf-ihrem-feedreader-4476976.html)

6. **Special TV note.** RTL Aktuell’s broadcast reports may exist as video pages, while the article tested above is written text on RTL.de. RTL+ also carried a 19 September 2024 audio update about the attacks. [RTL+ update](https://plus.rtl.de/was-jetzt-p_19169/audio/update-was-uber-die-explosionen-im-libanon-bekannt-ist-c_573124)

## ntv

1. **Identity in 2024.** News articles were published on `n-tv.de`, with sections such as `/politik/` and `/der_tag/`. This was separate from RTL.de, despite common RTL Deutschland ownership.

2. **Test URL.** [“Tausende Pager explodiert: Hisbollah schwört Israel Rache”](https://www.n-tv.de/politik/Hisbollah-schwoert-Israel-Rache-article25235207.html), published 18 September 2024 at 07:34.

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** None identified for ordinary ntv.de news. The site was ad-supported, and breaking-news articles were generally free. Any restricted special products or registration gates within the Lebanon corpus are UNVERIFIED.

4. **Robots/TDM.** Current robots.txt allows ordinary crawlers except specific paths such as search and archive pages. GPTBot, Google-Extended and CCBot are disallowed from the site. No explicit German TDM-reservation text was found in the file. [ntv robots.txt](https://www.n-tv.de/robots.txt)

5. **Enumeration hooks.** Strong enumeration potential: article URLs expose sequential-looking IDs such as `article25235207`, and robots.txt exposes several sitemaps, including the main sitemap, video sitemap, section sitemap and `news.xml`. The site also has an `/archiv/` path, though robots.txt disallows it. [ntv robots.txt](https://www.n-tv.de/robots.txt)

## BILD

1. **Identity in 2024.** Articles were published on `bild.de`, commonly under `/politik/ausland-und-internationales/`. The URL structure used a 26-character article identifier, for example `66e983275eec914fde514474`.

2. **Test URL.** [“Hunderte Hisbollah-Piepser detonieren gleichzeitig: Explosionswelle schockt Libanon”](https://www.bild.de/politik/ausland-und-internationales/dutzende-terroristen-verletzt-etliche-hisbollah-piepser-explodiert-66e983275eec914fde514474), published 17 September 2024 at 22:22. It is accessible without a BILDplus subscription.

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** Mixed model. BILDplus was a hard gate for marked premium articles, while many breaking-news and agency-based articles remained free. BILD’s own product information says that many current news articles remain free without an account. The exact proportion of Lebanon coverage marked BILDplus is UNVERIFIED. [BILDplus FAQ](https://www.bild.de/sonstiges/faq-zu-bild-produkten/maximieren-sie-ihr-bildplus-erlebnis-hier-erfahren-sie-alles-ueber-bildplus-premium-673c8a844df82f515063cb0f)

4. **Robots/TDM.** Current robots.txt blocks a large named list of AI and scraping bots from the entire site. It allows ordinary crawling with selected path exclusions and advertises multiple sitemaps. It links to a separate licensing file, `rsl.xml`, but that file could not be fetched for verification. [BILD robots.txt](https://www.bild.de/robots.txt)

5. **Enumeration hooks.** Very strong. BILD exposes a dated archive page with a date parameter, for example [17 September 2024](https://www.bild.de/themen/uebersicht/archiv/archiv-82532020.bild.html?archiveDate=2024-09-17). It also exposes `sitemap-news.xml`, `sitemap-video.xml`, a sitemap index and category sitemap. [BILD robots.txt](https://www.bild.de/robots.txt)

## DER SPIEGEL

1. **Identity in 2024.** Articles were published on `spiegel.de`. The former Spiegel Online brand had already been consolidated under the DER SPIEGEL name and domain by 2020. Article URLs use UUID-style identifiers beginning with `a-`, rather than simple sequential numeric IDs.

2. **Test URL.** [“Hunderte Pager in Hosentaschen von Hisbollah-Mitgliedern explodiert”](https://www.spiegel.de/ausland/israel-gaza-krieg-hisbollah-mitglieder-durch-explodierende-pager-in-beirut-und-im-sueden-libanons-verwundet-a-8d9a237f-fdeb-4377-9394-9f0be7783816), published 17 September 2024 at 16:42. The article is accessible in the current indexed version.

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** Mixed hard-paywall model. SPIEGEL+ articles were restricted to subscribers, while many breaking-news reports remained free. The test article is a free breaking-news item. The share of the Lebanon corpus behind SPIEGEL+ is UNVERIFIED. SPIEGEL describes SPIEGEL+ as providing access to all premium digital content. [SPIEGEL+ description](https://gruppe.spiegel.de/spiegel-media/werbetraeger/der-spiegel-digital/spiegel)

4. **Robots/TDM.** `https://www.spiegel.de/robots.txt` was blocked from retrieval by the search tool. Current crawler rules and any explicit TDM or AI-training reservation are therefore UNVERIFIED. The domain’s robots restriction itself is a risk flag for automated collection.

5. **Enumeration hooks.** Article UUIDs provide stable identifiers. Search and topic pages can enumerate indexed material, but a complete dated article archive or public September-October 2024 sitemap was not verified. The UUID format is useful for deduplication, not chronological enumeration.

## t-online

1. **Identity in 2024.** The domain was `t-online.de`. The brand had dropped “.de” from its logo and marketing years earlier, but the article domain remained `t-online.de`. Article URLs used paths such as `/nachrichten/ausland/.../id_100492044/...`.

2. **Test URL.** [“Hisbollah-Pager im Libanon explodiert: Darum soll Israel jetzt angegriffen haben”](https://www.t-online.de/nachrichten/ausland/internationale-politik/id_100492044/hisbollah-pager-im-libanon-explodiert-darum-soll-israel-jetzt-angegriffen-haben.html), published 18 September 2024 and credited “Von dpa.”

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** None for editorial access. t-online offered a PUR subscription for an ad- and tracking-reduced experience, not for unlocking news. Its editorial principles explicitly described the news offer as free and said it did not restrict access through paywalls. [t-online editorial principles](https://www.t-online.de/ueber-t-online/id_100280284/journalistische-prinzipien-so-arbeitet-die-redaktion-von-t-online.html) and [PUR description](https://www.t-online.de/ueber-t-online/id_90252234/pur-abo-nachrichten-auf-t-online-ohne-werbung-lesen.html)

4. **Robots/TDM.** Current robots.txt allows ordinary article crawling with selected exclusions. GPTBot, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot are blocked, with limited finance-section exceptions. The file expressly reserves commercial TDM rights under §44b UrhG and prohibits automated access without permission. [t-online robots.txt](https://www.t-online.de/robots.txt)

5. **Enumeration hooks.** Strong sequential-ID hook. Article URLs expose IDs such as `id_100492044`. A complete dated archive was not verified, but the ID system and search-engine indexing make date-window collection practical. The robots file does not expose a public sitemap in the fetched version.

## RND

1. **Identity in 2024.** Articles were published on `rnd.de`. The site had already relaunched under the RND brand in 2022, with regional media integrations continuing around it. Article URLs use a readable slug plus a 26-character alphanumeric identifier.

2. **Test URL.** [“Pager-Explosionen im Libanon: Funkgeräte wohl mit Sprengstoff bestückt”](https://www.rnd.de/politik/pager-explosionen-im-libanon-funkgeraete-wohl-mit-sprengstoff-bestueckt-7GX5G5II65JA5OFN6DWYOR24LI.html), published 18 September 2024 at 07:20 and credited `RND/dpa`.

3. **Paywall model in 2024.** Mixed model. RND+ existed for premium analyses, interviews, background and opinion pieces. The tested agency-based breaking-news article was publicly readable. The share of Lebanon coverage in RND+ is UNVERIFIED. [RND launch announcement describing RND+](https://www.rnd.de/medien/willkommen-auf-den-neuen-seiten-des-rnd-MWR4HNHCLZF33ACDCYSZY224GQ.html)

4. **Robots/TDM.** Current robots.txt allows most article pages to ordinary crawlers but blocks GPTBot, CCBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and others. It expressly reserves commercial TDM rights and requires permission for automated collection. [RND robots.txt](https://www.rnd.de/robots.txt)

5. **Enumeration hooks.** Strong dated archive hook. RND exposes pages such as [the 18 September 2024 archive](https://www.rnd.de/archiv/artikel-18-09-2024/). Article IDs are stable but not obviously chronological. The archive page is the best route for date-bounded enumeration.

4. **Robots/TDM.** A dpa robots.txt file could not be reliably fetched. Current crawler/TDM rules are UNVERIFIED.
