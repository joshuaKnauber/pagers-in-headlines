## Verdict

- Germany: Conditional pass. The main routes work, but the register overstates archive reliability and has unresolved evidence gaps around ZDF and WELT.
- US: Fail pending correction. The NYT plan makes an unsupported general claim, and the live-blog fallback has not been shown to be sufficiently rich or enumerable.
