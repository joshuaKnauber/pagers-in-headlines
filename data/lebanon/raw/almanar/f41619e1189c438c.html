The Leader and the Wounded Hezbollah Fighter

6 mins ago September 19, 2024
Only Pictures

A Hezbollah member, who was wounded in pager blasts on Tuesday, listens to the speech of the group’s Secretary General Sayyed Hasan Nasrallah from Hospital.

In his speech, Sayyed Nasrallah  affirmed the unwavering determination of the wounded members to return to the frontlines.

His eminence warned Israeli officials against any incursion into Lebanese territory.

“You will be met by hundreds of fighters who were injured on Tuesday and Wednesday because they became more determined.”
