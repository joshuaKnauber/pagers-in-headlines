## Evidence summary

The most defensible backbone is:

- **Print/radio:** Kantar TGI Israel, annual 2024, Jewish adults 18+.
- **Linear TV:** Israeli Audience Research Board (“mador”) annual 2024 averages, supplemented by event-day reports.
- **Digital news:** 2024 Israeli Democracy Institute survey for audience reach; Ahrefs July 2025 for comparable site-ranking estimates.
- **Platforms:** DataReportal Digital 2025, mostly advertising-reach estimates rather than active users.

### Interpretation and gaps

- **TGI print figures are exposure, not circulation or unique readership.** The 2024 annual TGI database covers Jewish adults aged 18+; it is not a full all-resident Israel measure. Calcalist’s figure combines its standalone edition with its Yedioth supplement, with overlap removed.
- **TV ratings are not population percentages in the same sense as TGI exposure.** They are panel-based viewing ratings, generally for the 20:00–21:30 main-news slot. The 2024 war effect is clearest for Channel 14: its main-news average rose from **6.2% to 7.4%** year-on-year, while Channel 12 remained dominant.
- **Digital rankings are split between survey reach and modeled traffic.** IDI gives self-reported news-source use; Ahrefs gives estimated organic-search visits, not total visits, direct traffic, app usage, or deduplicated users. This is why the two digital measures should not be merged.
- **The 2024 Israeli Internet Association summary identifies Ynet first, Mako second and Walla third among local sites, with Haaretz rising to fourth; it also reports increased visits to Russian-language sites such as Mignews and Newsru during the war.** [Source](https://www.ynet.co.il/digital/technews/article/s13ljn7xa)
- **Arabic-language measurement is the largest census gap.** Public 2024 evidence is mostly small-sample or source-type-level. It supports including Makan 33 and pan-Arab sources in the coding frame, but does not provide reliable outlet-by-outlet Israeli reach for Makan 33, Nas Radio, Hala TV, Al Jazeera Arabic or similar services.
- **English-language outlets are primarily export-facing.** The Jerusalem Post explicitly reports that 60% of readers are in the United States; therefore its global web traffic should not be treated as domestic Israeli consumption. Comparable Israel-only figures for Times of Israel and i24’s English service were not publicly available in the reviewed sources.
- **DataReportal numbers are ad-planning estimates, not active-user counts.** DataReportal specifically warns that platform estimates may include duplicate accounts, revisions and non-human accounts, especially for X. [Methodological notes](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-israel)
- **For a September 2024 pager-attack census, use the annual figures as the structural backbone, then add event-window measures**—daily TV ratings, website traffic spikes and platform analytics for 17–19 September 2024—because war-period behavior was demonstrably volatile.
