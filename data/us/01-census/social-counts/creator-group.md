### Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2025

The 2025 report provides unusually direct evidence of creator-led news reach in the United States:

- **Joe Rogan: 22%** of the U.S. sample said they encountered news or commentary from him during the week after the inauguration.
- **Tucker Carlson: 14%** reported encountering his news or commentary in the same period.
- The report also names **Megyn Kelly, Candace Owens, Ben Shapiro, Brian Tyler Cohen, and David Pakman** among other widely accessed personalities, without giving all of them a percentage in the summary text.
- Reuters describes the broader trend as the growth of a personality-driven alternative-media sector, particularly through YouTube and TikTok.

Sources: [Reuters executive summary](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/dnr-executive-summary), [2025 report PDF](https://memo98.sk/storage/media/2025/06/17/e0fe8cbb-5874-4493-baa6-d4f5f5833d13.pdf).

Important caveat: these Reuters figures measure whether respondents **came across news or commentary**, not whether they deliberately sought out the creator, subscribed, or watched a complete program.
